## Melbourne Fire Brigade 0.2.0
- Voice control for the map. Press your listen key and say a postal, or say "Fire Com dispatch" — the dispatcher answers "Go for Dispatch" — and the map goes straight there. The dispatcher reads the postal back with its nearest road ("Copy, postal three six one… Palomino Avenue"). Works while you're in game.
- Dispatcher voices: Ann, plus 5 female and 5 male natural-sounding voices, and an optional GTA-style police-scanner radio effect.
- Settings → Voice: pick your microphone (with a live level bar), choose key only or key + phrase, set your listen key (any key or a mouse side button), pick the voice, and turn the postal read-back on or off.
- Voice control waits until a microphone is found. The speech engine downloads once (about 56 MB) the first time it's turned on.
