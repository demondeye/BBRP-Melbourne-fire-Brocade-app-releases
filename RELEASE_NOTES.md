## Melbourne Fire Brigade 0.2.0
- Voice control for the map. Press your listen key and say a postal, or say "Fire Com dispatch" — the dispatcher answers "Go for Dispatch" — and the map goes straight there. The dispatcher reads the postal back with its nearest road, and the corner or intersection when the map shows one ("Copy, postal five two four. The nearest road is Eclipse Boulevard. Intersection of Eclipse Boulevard and Milton Road"). After "Fire Com dispatch" or the listen key, say "repeat that" to hear the last postal again. Works while you're in game.
- 10 natural-sounding dispatcher voices (5 female, 5 male) and an optional GTA-style police-scanner radio effect.
- Settings → Voice: pick your microphone (with a live level bar), choose key only or key + phrase, set your listen key (any key or a mouse side button), pick the voice, turn the postal read-back on or off, and choose whether it calls out corners and intersections.
- Voice control waits until a microphone is found. The speech engine downloads once (about 56 MB) the first time it's turned on.
