## Melbourne Fire Brigade 0.1.0
- First fire service version, copied from the Victoria Ambulance app.
- New Map app: the whole city with every postal on it. Search or click a postal to see its nearest street and whether it's a corner, T-intersection or intersection (and of which streets). Right-click for a waypoint.
- Fire-red look and flame logo.
- Updates itself from this repo's Releases.
